**Rule:** every word must be reachable within 3 taps on both the 45 and the 84 board.

## Summary

| Board | Direct | Via Forms | Synonym | Not reachable |

| 45 | 308 | 6 | 4 | 0 |
| 84 | 308 | 6 | 4 | 0 |

Taps: a word on the main board is 1 tap; Words > Colors > red is 3 taps (Words button, page button, word); Forms from a main-board word is 3 taps (the word, Forms, the form).

## Synonyms used

Only four words may use a synonym: father = dad, mother = mom, rabbit = bunny, house = home. Used here: father = dad, house = home, mother = mom, rabbit = bunny.

## Reached only through Forms

These words have no button of their own; they are forms of a main-board word: ate, goes, going, got, made, went.
