# VOICE (Phase 1.8 part B)

**VOICE** stands for **Variable Output for Individualized Communication and Expression**. It is a free, offline communication board for children who are new to AAC
(augmentative and alternative communication). Built for iPad, in landscape.

## What it does
- A core board of **45 locations (9 x 5)** or **84 locations (12 x 7)**, chosen
  **per learner** (adult menu > Board size). On a given board size **a word never
  changes location**, on the main board or on any page. Words not yet revealed
  show as blank gray tiles, so visible words never move.
- The first four rows of the 84 board are identical to the 45 board (same words,
  same places). The 45 board's bottom row (yes, no, Chat and the category buttons)
  is the 84 board's bottom row, in the same columns. Rows E and F of the 84 board
  hold the extra words. Switching size moves the bottom row down, so it is a
  relearning event (the adult must confirm).
- Tap a word: it speaks immediately and appears in the message bar.
  Tap the message bar to speak the whole sentence (see **Speech pacing** below). Delete removes the last
  chip; Clear removes everything.
- **Category pages (two taps).** Tap Chat, Food, Drinks, Play, Places, People or
  Favorites (and, on the 84 board, About me, Feelings or Animals): the page opens
  (no speech) with an orange background and the page name at the top. Tap an item:
  it speaks, is added to the message bar, and the board returns to the main page.
  The orange **Back** button (left arrow) sits in the same spot the button that
  opened the page has. **Back goes up one level.**
  - **Row groups.** Every category page is organized as rows of related words (for example
    Food: Snacks, Fruit, Meals, Breakfast, Veggies and extras). A word keeps the **same row and
    the same column on both board sizes**: the 45 board uses columns 1-9 of a row, and the 84
    board adds a few more words in columns 10-12 of that same row. The Back button sits where
    the button that opened the page sits; when it falls inside a word row on the 45 board
    (Food and Chat), that column is left empty on both sizes. On the 84 board the two bottom
    rows of every page are left empty for your own words.
  - **Newer words appear from stage 3.** The original starter items of each page (for
    example cracker, apple, banana ... on Food) show whenever the page is open; the newer words
    in the rows show from stage 3 (or when you choose Always show on one). Nothing moves.
  - **Chat** (both sizes): Greetings, Turns and play, Comments, Manners and protest, Help.
    Each speaks its whole phrase ("I don't know" has the short label "don't know").
  - **Feelings** (feelings, body needs, body parts) and **Animals** (pets and farm, wild,
    small, more) are main-grid buttons on the 84 board. On the 45 board they are reached from
    a page: Chat > Feelings and Play > Animals (three taps to a word; the Back button goes up
    one level; "Make this a page" is not offered there).
  - **Toy folders and animal folders (Phase 1.7B).** Everything that was on the Play and Animals pages
    is exactly where it was; a row of **folder buttons** was added in the empty row 5 of each page.
    - **Play** has six toy folders in row 5, columns 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 (the same columns on both board sizes;
      column 1 is Animals and column 4 is Back on the 45 board): **Sensory, Pretend, Building, Vehicles, Art, Music**.
    - **Animals** has eight animal folders in row 5, columns 2-9 (column 1 is Back on the 45 board): **Pets, Farm, Zoo,
      Ocean, Bugs, Birds, Dinosaurs, Forest**.
    - A folder is a page with one row of words: nine in columns 1-9 (the 45 board), and three more in columns 10-12 on
      the 84 board. Its Back button sits where the folder button is. The words are new "newer words" (they show from
      stage 3, like the other words on pages, and the folder buttons do too). A word that is in several folders
      (dog in Pets, bear in Zoo and Forest) is one word with one photo, one rename and one show/hide setting.
    - **Depth rule for built-in folders (generalized in Phase 1.8A).** A built-in folder page may sit one level below a
      category page or a Words page, so a folder word can be **four taps** deep: Play > Animals > Farm > cow on the 45 board
      (Animals is opened from Play there), and Food > More food > Desserts > cake on both sizes. On the 84 board a folder
      under a main-grid category is three taps (Animals > Farm > cow). Back always goes up one level at a time. An adult's
      "Make this a page" limit is unchanged (offered only on a page at depth 1) and is **never offered on a built-in folder
      page** (an adult can still add a word to an empty cell there). In Model mode the ring shows the whole path in order
      after a word is chosen (up to four steps).
    - Some symbols are weaker stand-ins (see CREDITS.md for each picture's source): fourteen simple pictures
      were drawn for this project because no open symbol exists (digger, spinner, slime, stamps, shaker, tambourine,
      triceratops, stegosaurus, pterodactyl, raptor, ankylosaurus, spinosaurus, dino egg, fossil). An adult can
      replace any of them with a real photo (Edit board > photo).
  - **Folders on the category pages (Phase 1.8A).** The same folder format (nine words in row 1, three more on the 84 board, Back where the folder
    button is, rows 6-7 left empty on the 84 board) now exists on six more pages. Nothing that was there moved; each folder button
    sits in a cell that was empty, in the same columns on both board sizes:
    - **Places** (row 5, columns 1-8): In the house (was Home), School, Stores, Food places, Fun places, Outdoors, Travel, Health.
    - **People** (row 5, columns 1-4 and 6): Family, School people, Helpers, Pretend characters (generic only, no named
      characters), and **Friends**, an empty folder an adult fills with the learner's own friends (Edit board > tap a cell > Add a word).
    - **Chat** (row 5, columns 6-9): Questions, Requests, Play talk, Compliments. **Feelings** (row 4, columns 1-6): More feelings,
      Sick and hurt, Calm down, Sensory, Body care, Body parts. **Drinks** (row 4, columns 1-6): Juice, Milk and shakes, Hot drinks,
      Water and sports, Party drinks, Drink things.
    - **Food:** the last button of row 5 (it was "ranch") is now **More food**, a folder of eight folders: Desserts, Sauces and
      spreads, Fast food, Lunch and dinner, More snacks, More fruit, More veggies, Dairy. It is the only existing cell that changed:
      **ranch** (same word, so its renamed label, photo and show/hide choice moved with it) is now in More food > Sauces and spreads,
      and the adult menu shows a note about it. More food's eight folder buttons are in **row 5** (columns 1-8, Back at column 9 on both
      sizes), not row 1, because a folder's Back button sits where its folder button is: buttons in row 1 would put Back in the row
      of the folder's own nine words.
    - A word that is in several folders (toilet in Home and Body care, milkshake in Milk and shakes and Fast food) is one word. New
      words and the new folder buttons show from stage 3. Sixty-one more simple pictures were drawn for this project where no
      open symbol exists; see CREDITS.md for every picture's source and the weak stand-ins (for example "mango juice" shows a mango).
  - **Places > "In the house" (Phase 1.8B).** The folder that was labeled "Home" is now **In the house** with a room picture, so it
    no longer looks like the word "home" on the same page. It is the same folder (same place, same words).
  - **About me**: sentence buttons with a blank an adult fills in ("My name is ___",
    "I am ___ years old", "I go to ___", "My mom is ___", "My dad is ___", "I love ___",
    "My birthday is ___", "My pet is ___", "My brother is ___", "My sister is ___", "My teacher is ___",
    "I am allergic to ___", "Emergency contact: ___") plus two fixed sentences ("I use this device
    to talk", "Please give me time to answer"). A button whose blank is empty stays a hidden gray
    tile until an adult fills it in (Edit board > tap it > **Fill in**). On the 45 board it is
    reached from the People page (People > About me, three taps); on the 84 board it is a
    main-grid button (two taps). There is no home-address button, and the emergency-contact
    field shows a privacy reminder.
  - **Places** includes "here" (it used to be on the main board).
- **Folders on the Words pages (Phase 1.8B).** The same folder format (nine words, three more on the 84 board, Back where the folder
  button is) now exists on six Words pages, in cells that were empty on both board sizes (rows and columns below start at 1):
  - **Describing** (row 4, columns 1-5): Opposites, Shapes, Textures, Taste and smell, Sizes and amounts.
  - **Time** (row 2, columns 5-9): Days, Months, Seasons and holidays, Routine times, Clock words.
  - **Numbers** (row 2, columns 6-8): Math words, Money, Big numbers. **Clothes** (row 2, columns 6-8): Accessories, Weather clothes,
    Getting dressed. **Outside** (row 2, columns 7-9): Weather, Space, Plants.
  - **Things** (row 4, columns 1-5): School supplies, Tech, Household, Bathroom things, Bedroom.
  - **Actions' five folders are on the Words menu** (row 3, columns 1-5; the menu has no Back cell): Daily routine, School actions, Play
    actions, Body moves, Kitchen. The Actions page itself has no empty row for them (one free cell only) and nothing may move. Words >
    Daily routine > wake up is three taps. The verb **march** is its own word (the month March is another).
  - A word is up to four taps deep (Words > Time > Days > Monday). The folder buttons and the new words show from stage 3. The month
    **May** and the season **fall** are separate words from the Dolch word "may" and the verb "fall" (the Forms button does not offer
    "fell" or "fallen" after the season). The days and months are small calendar pictures drawn for this project (the day or month is
    highlighted); see CREDITS.md for every picture's source. In all, 267 new words and 29 folder buttons were added: 167 Mulberry pictures, 55 OpenMoji pictures and 75 simple pictures drawn for this project (including the "In the house" folder picture). The weaker stand-ins are the abstract words: bland (a neutral face), later (an hourglass), none, less, a little, a lot, tiny and huge
    (drawn dots and circles), closed (a closed door), count and total (counters and an abacus), and the action pictures for get ready, wait my turn, share and tiptoe. Real photos can replace any of them.
- **Words pages (three taps).** The **Words** button in the top bar (between the message text and Delete)
  opens the Words menu: Little words, More little words, Actions, Past tense, Describing, Time (first row)
  and Colors, Numbers, Clothes, Outside, Things (second row). Tap a page, then a word: that is
  **Words, page, word = three taps**, and the board returns to the main page. While any Words page is
  open the same button reads **Close** and goes straight back to the main board. A page's orange
  **Back** button sits where its button on the Words menu sits and goes up one level (to the menu).
  The pages use the same row-group layout as the category pages (45 board: columns 1-9; the 84 board adds
  columns 10-12 of the same rows; rows 6-7 of the 84 board are left empty for your words), so a word has the
  same row and column on both sizes and **never moves**.
  - **Every one of the 318 Dolch sight words is reachable within 3 taps on both board sizes**
    (`docs/dolch-coverage.md` shows exactly how each one is reached; `python3 tools/dolch-coverage.py` checks it
    and fails if any word is not). Only four synonyms are allowed: father = dad, mother = mom,
    rabbit = bunny, house = home. Six words (ate, goes, going, got, made, went) are reached through **Forms**.
  - **Little words** are text first: the word is drawn large across the button, with a symbol only where one is
    genuinely clear (over, under, around, away). The button and its picture area are exactly the same size as
    any other button (the picture area is simply empty). **Pronouns** (he, she, we, they, me, him, her, us, them,
    myself, my, your, his, our, their, its) are on *More little words*, in the rows after the helper words
    (is, was, have, will...). *Past tense* holds ran, saw, said, came, gave and found. The extra pages
    (More little words, Past tense) exist because 44 buttons per page on the 45 board were not enough for all the
    little words and still leave room for the Back button.
  - The Words and Forms buttons are shown by the learner's **Show Words button** setting (adult menu >
    Vocabulary stage): Auto (from stage 3, the default), Always or Never.
- **Keyboard (ABC).** The **ABC** button in the top bar (between Forms and Delete, in the same place on both
  board sizes) opens a large QWERTY keyboard laid over the grid area. The message bar stays visible above it,
  and the grid underneath does not move or change size (the keyboard is simply drawn on top). Keys are lower
  case and at least 64 px tall on an iPad. The keys: letters, a wide **space**, **Backspace** (deletes one typed
  letter; with nothing typed it does nothing, and the top-bar **Delete** is still what removes a whole chip),
  **123** (switches to the number pad), an **apostrophe** (') key between 123 and Space, so "don't" and "I'm" can be
  typed (the letter keys did not change size or place), **Done** and **Close**.
  - Typing builds a **draft chip** (dashed outline) at the end of the message bar. **Space** turns it into a
    normal chip (whose spoken text is the typed word) and says it; the keyboard stays open for the next word.
    **Done** does the same and closes the keyboard. **Close** (or tapping ABC again) with a non-empty draft also
    finalizes it, so typing is never lost. An empty draft is just thrown away. Switching with **123** finalizes
    the draft first. Opening the adult menu keeps the draft as a chip but says nothing.
  - **Touch:** the keyboard uses ordinary taps. Hold to select and Ignore repeated taps do not apply to it
    (typing needs quick taps); instead each key ignores a second hit within 120 ms, which filters out
    accidental double touches. The same letter twice ("book") works at normal typing speed.
  - **Speak each letter while typing** (per learner, default Off): each key says its letter name ("ay", "bee",
    "see"...). The names are spelled out in `LETTER_NAMES` in `js/keyboard.js` because a bare "a" is read as the word "a".
  - **Show keyboard button** (adult menu > Vocabulary stage, next to Show Words button): Auto (from stage 3, the
    default), Always or Never. When it is Never, the number pad has no ABC key either.
  - **Model mode:** key presses ring briefly (the learner's ring settings), and each finalized typed word is logged
    as `{ word, mode, source: "keyboard" }` (with the prompt info in Model mode like any model event).
- **Number pad.** Digits 0-9 in a large 3 x 4 layout (1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 / Backspace 0 Done) in the same overlay
  frame, with **Close** and **ABC** (back to the keyboard). Opened from the keyboard's **123** key, or from the new
  **Number pad** button on the **Numbers** page (Words > Numbers): it sits in row 2, column 5 of that page on both
  board sizes, an empty cell before, so no other button moved.
  - Digits build a draft chip ("2", "24"). **Done** finalizes it: the chip shows the digits ("24") and the spoken
    text is the English words ("twenty-four"), and it is spoken. 0 to 999,999, with "one hundred five" and "two thousand
    twenty-six" (no "and"). Leading zeros are dropped (0, 0, 7 gives 7). A 7th digit is refused (the key flashes red).
    A number opened from the Numbers page returns the board to the main page when it is finalized, like any word chosen
    on a page. The words come from `js/numberwords.js`; `node tools/test-numberwords.mjs` runs its tests.
  - Model mode logs `{ word: "24", mode, source: "numberpad", spoken: "twenty-four" }`. The Number pad button shows the
    ring first and the pad opens a moment later, so the child can see where the button is.
- **Speech pacing.** Speed for single words and speed for sentences are separate (adult menu > Voice):
  - **Speed** is for a single word (a word button, a letter, a form, a typed one-word chip).
  - **Sentence speed** (0.5 to 1.5, default 0.9) is for the message bar and for anything of two or more words:
    phrase buttons ("thank you", the Chat phrases, About me sentences), phrases an adult adds, edited spoken text of
    several words, a typed number such as "one hundred five", and the Hear it buttons in the editor. Sentences sound
    faster than the same words said one at a time, so they get their own, slower setting.
  - **Pause between words**: Off (default), Short 0.25 s, Medium 0.5 s or Long 0.8 s. Off: tapping the message bar says
    the whole sentence as one utterance at Sentence speed (tapping again while it plays stops it). On: each chip is
    its own utterance, in order, with the gap after each (a chip of several words, such as "thank you" or a custom phrase,
    is said as one piece, still at Sentence speed). The chip being said is highlighted. **Tapping the bar again while it
    plays stops it**; tapping a word, Delete or Clear also stops it.
  - **Hear a sample sentence** says "I want more bubbles please" the way the message bar would (with the pauses if on).
    **Preview a word** says "bubbles" at word speed.
  - Reset to child / adult default changes only pitch and word speed; Sentence speed and the pause are left alone.
- **Forms.** The **Forms** button (next to Words) is on only when the last word in the message bar has other
  forms: verbs (go: goes, going, went, gone), irregular verbs, adjectives (good, better, best), plurals
  (man, men), pronouns (I, me, my, mine, myself). It opens a large popover over the grid (the grid does not
  change); tap a form to replace the last word and speak it. Tap outside to close. The forms live in
  `data/word-forms.json`; add a family there to add more. In Model mode, Forms counts as a model event
  (`{ word, form, mode: "model" }`).
- **Word-forms dial (BETA only for now).** Press and hold a word that has forms (go, eat, big, dog, I...) for about
  0.5 s and its forms open around the button on a see-through layer (the grid does not move). The word stays in the
  centre. Nothing is spoken when the dial opens. A quick tap is exactly as before (speaks on release, no delay), and a word
  with no forms behaves as a normal tap even when held. **Two ways to choose:** *slide* (keep the finger down, slide onto a form,
  it lights up, lift) or *tap* (lift without sliding, then tap a form). Tapping outside, or sliding onto a form and then back to the
  middle or off the dial before lifting, cancels. Choosing speaks the form (one word: Speed), puts it in the message bar and logs
  `{ word, form, id, mode }`, like the Forms button. From a category page the board then returns to the main board, as for any word.
  Only the first word of a family has a dial (go, not went; I, not me).
  - **Fixed slots (motor planning):** a form of a given type always sits in the same direction, for every word. 8 compass slots; a slot
    with no form is simply empty:

    | Word type | N (up) | NE | E (right) | SE | S (down) | SW | W (left) | NW |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | Verb | past (went) | negative (don't) | -ing (going) | extra | -s (goes) | extra | past participle (gone) | extra |
    | Describing word | | -est (biggest) | -er (bigger) | | | | | |
    | Noun | | | plural (dogs) | | | | | |
    | Pronoun | | | object (me, him, her, us, them) | other (mine, yours, hers, ours, theirs) | possessive (my, his, your, our, their, its) | | reflexive (myself...) | |

    Extras (am / are / were for "be") use the first free slot in the order NW, SE, SW, NE, S, W, N, E. this / that: these / those count as plurals (E).
    The map is in `js/forms.js` (`TYPE_SLOT`); change it there.
  - **Edges:** the dial is a compact 3 x 3 arrangement of big chips (at least 64 px tall; about 72 to 77 px on an iPad). Every chip keeps its
    direction from the centre word. Near a screen edge the whole dial (centre word included) is nudged inward by the smallest amount so every chip
    is fully on screen and nothing covers the centre word (the gaps only shrink if it still would not fit).
  - **Markers:** each chip has a small picture for its type (arrow back = past, arrow to a line = past participle, motion lines = -ing, + = -s,
    two dots = plural, no sign = negative, bars = -er / -est, person pictures = pronouns). Per learner, *Show form markers* (default On; turn Off for
    readers). It is independent of *Show words only*.
  - **Settings (adult menu > Vocabulary stage, under Show Words button), per learner and in backups:** *Word forms dial* Auto (stage 3 and up, default)
    / Always / Never; *Hold time for dial* 0.3 to 1.0 s (default 0.5); *Show form markers*.
  - **With the touch settings:** with *Hold to select* on, the word is selected after the hold-to-select time as usual and the dial opens 0.8 s later
    if the finger is still down (releasing between the two times just selects the word; choosing a form then replaces that word in the message bar,
    cancelling keeps it). The dial's own hold-time slider is not used then. Choosing a form counts as a selection for *Ignore repeated taps*;
    opening the dial does not. Choices inside the dial are not themselves subject to hold-to-select or the ignore window. If hold-to-select has
    already taken the child back to the main board from a category page, no dial opens.
  - **Other modes:** no dial in Edit mode. In Model mode an adult hold shows the dial; the chosen form gets the ring / critter prompt and is logged
    as a model event with the form.
- **Reading stage (words only).** Adult menu > Vocabulary stage > *Show words only (no pictures)*: hides every
  symbol and photo on every page and shows the words large and centered in the same buttons (colors stay).
  Saved per learner and included in backups.
- Several learners can share one iPad. Each has their own stage, hidden/shown
  words, renamed words, photos, added words and voice settings.
- Works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
  Photos and settings stay on the device.

Every button shows an open-licensed symbol (Mulberry Symbols or OpenMoji, see
CREDITS.md). Symbols for objects and food are stand-ins until an adult adds real
photos. Not built yet: photo-suggested words, data logging.

## Getting started (quick setup)
The very first time the app opens on a device (and whenever you tap **Add learner** in the adult menu) a
full-screen **Set up this board** card appears, with an optional learner name (default "Learner 1") and three big choices:
- **Full board - 84 buttons:** 84 board, stage 4 (every word shown).
- **Full board - 45 buttons:** 45 board, stage 4 (every word shown).
- **New AAC learner:** 45 board, stage 1 (6 words; reveal more as the learner grows).

Words and keyboard buttons are left on Auto. Everything can be changed later in the adult menu. On a brand-new
device there is also a small **Restore from a backup file** link, so you can load a prepared board (see Backup).
The passcode is not asked for here; you create it the first time you open the adult menu. Devices that already
have learners never see this card on start, and no learner's stage is changed by this update.

**Staying unlocked.** The passcode screen has a **Stay unlocked for 10 minutes** box (on by default). After a
correct passcode, the next opening of the adult menu on that device skips the passcode for 10 minutes (the 3-second
hold is still needed every time). Untick the box to be asked every time.

## For adults
**Open the adult menu:** press and HOLD the small faint circle at the top-left of
the gray strip (under the message bar) for 3 seconds. A ring fills while you hold.
Then type your 4-digit passcode (unless you are still unlocked, see above).

**First time:** you are asked to create a passcode (type it twice). This only keeps
little fingers out; it is not security. If it is forgotten it can only be reset by
clearing this app's website data in iPad Settings, which ALSO erases all learners and
photos. Keep a recent backup (see Backup below), then restore it after resetting.

**Adult menu**
- **Learner:** switch, add (shows the quick setup card), rename or delete a learner (the last one cannot be deleted).
  The current learner's first name shows faintly in the message bar corner.
- **Board size (per learner):** 45 or 84 locations. Switching asks you to confirm and
  explains that word locations change (a relearning event). The learner keeps their stage.
  See "Where your changes are stored" below for what comes with them.
- **Vocabulary stage:** tap Stage 1-4. Each stage lists the words it reveals on this
  learner's board. Below the stages: **Show Words button** (Auto from stage 3 / Always / Never) and
  **Show words only (no pictures)** (the reading stage: pictures are hidden so the learner reads the words).
- **Edit board:** see every button (hidden ones faded with a dashed outline) on the
  page you are on. Tap any button to change it: Follow stage / Always show / Always hide,
  label, spoken text, and a photo (take or choose one; it is shrunk and kept on the
  device). A large **speaker button** ("Hear it") beside the Label, Spoken text, Fill in and
  page name fields says the text you have typed so far, exactly as the board would (this learner's
  voice, pitch and speed). It does not save anything, does not touch the message bar and is not
  recorded. Tap an **empty** cell to add a word there (this is how Favorites is filled);
  it keeps that spot forever. There is no way to move a word. Tap **Done** to leave.
  In Edit mode, tap a category button and choose "Open this page" to edit its page.
  The Edit banner also has a **Words** button (when this learner's Words button is allowed) that opens the Words menu, so the
  Words pages can be edited the same way (tap a page button, choose "Open this page"; the button then reads **Close Words**).
  - **Make this a page** (up to three taps deep). When you add a button to an empty cell
    on a page that is not already two levels down, choose **Make this a page**, give it a
    name (and optionally a photo). The new button looks like a category button and opens a
    new empty page of the learner's board size, with Back in the same spot as the button.
    Fill that page's empty cells with words. The main board is level 0, a page opened
    from it is level 1 (two taps to a word), a page opened from that is level 2 (three
    taps). "Make this a page" is not offered on a level-2 page. The built-in About me page
    under People (45 board) is level 2. The main board has no empty cells, so the first
    adult-made page is made on a page such as Favorites (making it level 2).
  - To delete an adult-made page, edit its button and choose **Delete this page**
    (asks first). The page and everything on it are removed.
  - Tapping a word on any page returns to the main board. In model mode the pages are
    stepped back out one at a time, showing the Model prompt on the button that opened each page, so the
    whole path (word, page button, category button) is shown.
- **Voice:** pick a voice, set pitch, speed (single words), Sentence speed and Pause between words,
  hear a word or a sample sentence, or reset pitch and speed to the child or adult default. Saved per learner.
  See **Speech pacing** above.
- **Touch (per learner):** two optional helps, both Off by default. They apply to the
  child's board (words, category buttons, Back) in normal and modeling mode, not to
  the message bar, Delete/Clear, adult screens or Edit mode.
  - *Hold to select* (0.1 to 2.0 s): the child must keep a finger on the button that
    long. A ring fills while holding; lifting early or sliding off does nothing.
  - *Ignore repeated taps* (0.25 to 3.0 s): after a word is spoken, taps on any board
    button are ignored for that long. Opening a category does not start the wait.
- **Backup:** *Save backup of this learner* or *of all learners* makes one .json file
  (board size, stage, button changes, pages you made, voice including Sentence speed and the pause, touch settings, Model prompt
  settings, the Words button, keyboard button and Speak-each-letter settings, the words-only setting, and photos, including the critter picture; never the passcode). Backups made by earlier versions still restore (they are upgraded: an older backup gets keyboard button Auto, letters not
  spoken, Sentence speed 0.9 and no pauses; a backup without the word-forms dial settings gets the defaults). In beta the backup file is version 10
  (the dial settings were added); the stable app refuses version 10 files until this is promoted. On the
  iPad a save sheet opens: choose **Save to Files**. *Restore from backup* reads a file,
  shows the learners and photo count inside, and for each learner asks whether to **add
  as a new learner** (default) or **replace** a learner with the same name (asks again
  before replacing). Nothing changes until you tap Restore.
- **Start modeling:** a purple "Modeling" bar appears. Your taps work normally and the
  tapped button gets the learner's *Model prompt* (see below); after a category item, the
  page stays up briefly so the prompt on the item is seen, then each button that opened a
  page gets the prompt in turn, so the whole path is shown. Hold **End** for 2 seconds to stop.
- **Model prompt (for this learner):** what Model mode shows on each modeled button.
  - *Prompt style:* **Ring only** (the default), **Critter only**, or **Critter and ring**.
  - *Ring opacity* and *Critter opacity*, 0 to 100% in steps of 5 (default 100%). At 0% that
    prompt is off, but the model is still recorded.
  - *Critter:* choose the animal (puppy, kitten, bunny, frog, owl), its color (8 swatches),
    an accessory (none, hat, bow, glasses), its size (small, medium or large: about 45%, 65%
    or 85% of a button's shorter side) and how it arrives: **hop** (arcs in from just off the
    bottom left of the screen), **peek** (slides up from the button's bottom edge) or
    **appear** (fades in). It stays about 1.2 seconds, then fades out. If the iPad is set to
    Reduce Motion, it always simply appears.
  - *Use my own picture:* a photo (shrunk to 512 px, kept only on this iPad) replaces the
    drawn animal. Pick something the child loves, like a favorite toy. *Remove picture* goes
    back to the animal. The animal, color and accessory choices do not apply to your own picture.
  - *Preview* shows the critter at the chosen opacity. *Preview on board* closes the menu for a
    moment, runs the prompt on the "more" button, and reopens the menu. Nothing is spoken,
    nothing is added to the message bar, and nothing is recorded.
  - **Why opacity fading helps:** a model prompt that stays just as bright forever can become
    the thing the child watches. Lowering the ring or critter opacity a little at a time makes
    the prompt less and less noticeable as the child succeeds, which is a built-in way to fade
    the prompt (for example 100%, 80%, 60% ... 0%) while you keep modeling the word's location.
  - **Caution:** if the child watches the critter more than the button, fade it faster (lower the
    opacity sooner) or switch to the ring. The critter is only meant to point at the button.
  - The ring and critter are drawn on top of the board and never block a tap, and they never move,
    resize or reflow any button.

## Where your changes are stored (rules)
Every change is kept in one of two places, so nothing lands on the wrong word:
- **Built-in words** (everything in the board file, including "want", "Chat", every
  Chat phrase, every About me button): changes are stored **by the word's id** - its
  renamed label, spoken text, show/hide choice, filled-in blank and photo. They
  **follow the word** to its place on the other board size. (One word is one id
  everywhere it appears: for example "he" on the People page and "he" on the 84 main
  board are the same word and share their changes.)
- **Things an adult adds to an empty cell** (a word on Favorites, an adult-made page and
  everything on it) are stored **by position and board size**. They belong to that cell
  on that board size only: a word added to Favorites on the 45 board is not on the 84
  Favorites page, and comes back if you switch back to 45. Adult-made pages exist only
  on the board size they were made on.
- Photos are stored the same way (a built-in word's photo by word, an added button's
  photo by position).
- **Upgrading older learners.** Before Phase 1.5A everything was stored by position,
  and "here" sat where "Chat" now sits. On first start, old data is re-keyed: whatever
  belonged to "here" (rename, show/hide, photo) moves with "here" to the Places page and
  nothing carries onto Chat. The same upgrade is applied to old backup files.
- **Phase 1.6 reorganized every category page into row groups.** Built-in words moved, and
  their renames, photos and show/hide choices moved with them (they are stored by word id).
  Where an adult had added a word to a cell that a built-in word now needs, **the adult's
  word is kept** and moved to the first free cell of the same page (on the 84 board the two
  bottom rows first). The 45 Food page is completely full, so such a word goes to the first free
  cell of Favorites instead. A page button you made moves with its page, and photos move too. A note listing
  every moved word appears at the top of the adult menu until you tap **Got it**. Backups made
  before this phase are upgraded the same way when restored. Code: `js/migrate.js`.
- **Phase 1.7 added the Number pad button** to the Numbers page (row 2, column 5). If an adult had added a word to that empty
  cell, the word is kept and moved to the next free cell of the page, with the same kind of note. Backups made before this
  version get the same check when restored.
- **Phase 1.7B added the folder buttons** (Play row 5, columns 2, 3, 5-8; Animals row 5, columns 2-9). An adult-added word in one of
  those previously empty cells is kept and moved to the next free cell of the same page (on the 84 board the two bottom rows
  first), with a note in the adult menu; a page button you made moves with its page, and photos move too. Learner data is now
  version 5 and backups version 7 (older backups get the same check when restored). Code: `js/migrate.js`.
- **Phase 1.8A added folder buttons** on Places (row 5, columns 1-8), People (row 5, columns 1-4 and 6), Chat (row 5, columns 6-9),
  Feelings and Drinks (row 4, columns 1-6), and the More food button on Food (replacing ranch). An adult-added word in one of those
  previously empty cells is kept and moved the same way (the 45 Places page is now full, so such a word goes to Favorites). Every
  learner saved before this version gets a note that **ranch moved into More food > Sauces and spreads**. Learner data is now version 6
  and backups version 8 (older backups get the same check, and the note, when restored). Code: `js/migrate.js`.
- **Phase 1.8B added folder buttons** on the Describing, Time, Numbers, Clothes, Outside and Things Words pages (see above). An
  adult-added word in one of those previously empty cells is kept and moved the same way, with a note. Learner data is now version 7
  and backups version 9 (older backups get the same check when restored). Code: `js/migrate.js`.

## How updates are tested (stable and beta)
The app comes in two copies on the same website, so clients never get an untested change:
- **Stable** (the main address, https://rwawrzonkowski-dotcom.github.io/AAC-App/) is what clients use. Its version label
  ("Stable 1.0"; it becomes "VOICE 1.1" and so on once VOICE is promoted) is shown small at the top of the adult menu and at the bottom of the setup screen.
- **Beta** (https://rwawrzonkowski-dotcom.github.io/AAC-App/beta/, folder `beta/`) is where new features are tried first.
  It shows "VOICE Beta 1.3" in the same two places and a small orange **BETA** tag in the corner of the message bar.
  It can be added to the home screen as its own icon ("VOICE Beta", the VOICE icon with a BETA band).

**Beta has completely separate storage.** Everything beta saves (learners, photos, passcode, "stay unlocked" time, settings)
starts with `beta-`, and stable keeps exactly the names it always used (database `aac-board`, settings `aac.*`). So beta
starts empty and shows the setup screen on its first open, and nothing done in beta can touch a client's data in stable.
To test with a real learner, make a backup in the stable app (adult menu > Backup) and use **Restore from a backup file** in beta
(backup files work in both). Offline copies are kept apart the same way: stable's is `aac-vNN`, beta's is `beta-aac-vNN`;
each service worker deletes only its own, and the stable one ignores everything under `beta/`.

**Beta-only features waiting to be promoted:** iPhone safe areas (the board and every full-screen layer keep clear of the camera cutout
and home bar; the same space is kept on both sides so buttons never move when the phone is turned), a "Test the volume" button with
device-specific tips (setup screen and adult menu > Voice), and a "45 buttons is easier on a phone" tip. On iPads and computers the
board is exactly as before. To test the safe areas on a desktop browser, set the CSS variables `--test-inset-l`, `--test-inset-r`,
`--test-inset-t`, `--test-inset-b` on `:root` (they stand in for the phone's real `env(safe-area-inset-*)` values; the app never sets them).

**Scripts** (run them from the project folder; they only change files on this computer and never commit or push):
- `tools/make-beta.sh` rebuilds `beta/` as a fresh copy of the stable app set up as beta. Use it to start testing a new round, or
  to throw away beta experiments. It overwrites `beta/`, so make beta changes only after running it. Running it twice changes
  nothing; if beta did change, its cache number goes up so devices fetch the new files.
- `tools/promote-beta.sh "VOICE 1.1"` makes the tested beta the new stable: it copies beta's app files over the main ones, keeps
  stable's own channel file, gives stable the names "VOICE" (manifest, page title), the unbanded VOICE icons from `tools/icon/` and
  `data/about.json`, sets the new version label, raises stable's cache number (`aac-v15` to
  `aac-v16`), rewrites the file list and prints which files changed. Then test, commit, and run `tools/make-beta.sh` to restart beta.
- `python3 tools/update-cache-list.py` (stable) and `python3 tools/update-cache-list.py beta` rewrite the file list in each `sw.js`
  (each lists only its own folder). Bump that copy's `CACHE_VERSION` after changing files.
- `tools/make-icons.py` draws the VOICE icon PNGs (180, 192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch-icon) from `tools/icon/voice-icon.svg`
  (needs cairosvg, or Playwright with Chromium). `tools/make-beta-icons.py` (needs Pillow) then redraws the BETA-band copies in
  `tools/beta-icons/`. Run both only if the icon artwork changes.

Typical round: `tools/make-beta.sh`, change files in `beta/`, test on an iPad at `/beta/`, then `tools/promote-beta.sh "VOICE 1.1"`.

## Filling in the About screen
The adult menu has an **About VOICE** button (at the bottom). It shows the full name, a one-line description, the creator, resource links,
a credits summary, the privacy note and the version. The creator and links are not in the code: they come from `data/about.json`
(in `beta/data/` for beta; `tools/promote-beta.sh` carries it to stable). Open it in any text editor and fill in the quotes:
```
{ "creatorName": "", "creatorCredentials": "", "creatorBio": "",
  "links": [ {"label": "Website", "url": ""}, {"label": "Resources", "url": ""},
             {"label": "Trainings", "url": ""}, {"label": "Contact", "url": ""} ] }
```
- `creatorName`, `creatorCredentials` (for example "M.S., BCBA") and `creatorBio` (a few sentences) are shown under "Creator".
  In the beta, while `creatorName` is empty, the screen shows the muted italic line "Creator name goes here" so you can see the spot.
- Each entry in `links` needs a `label` (the button text) and a full `url` starting with `https://` (a Contact link can be
  `mailto:you@example.com`). A link with an empty `url` shows as the muted line "Link coming soon: Website" instead of a button.
  These placeholders show **only in beta**; in stable, empty creator and empty links are left out entirely (no Resources heading if every link is empty).
  You can rename, remove or add entries. Links open in a new tab and need internet; the rest of the app stays fully offline.
- Keep it valid JSON (straight double quotes, commas between entries, none after the last). If the file is broken the placeholders show.
- After editing, raise `CACHE_VERSION` in that copy's `sw.js` (for example `beta-aac-v3` to `beta-aac-v4`) so devices fetch the new file.

## Files
- `data/core-board.json` the word map: every word (type, stage, picture), the 45 and 84
  main grids written out row by row, and the category pages as row groups ("words" in columns
  1-9, "adds" in columns 10-12 on the 84 board). Positions are permanent.
- `js/boardmodel.js` works out which word is in which cell for a grid size and a learner;
  `js/boarddata.js` loads the word map; `js/migrate.js` upgrades older learner data.
- `js/app.js` ties it together (pages, taps, edit and model modes, the Words and Forms buttons).
- `js/keyboard.js` the keyboard and number pad overlay (draft, finalizing, debounce); `js/numberwords.js` turns a number into
  English words (`tools/test-numberwords.mjs` tests it).
- `js/forms.js` loads `data/word-forms.json`, shows the Forms popover and holds the dial's slot map; `js/dial.js` draws the
  press-and-hold word-forms dial (beta) and reads the finger (slide or tap).
- `js/board.js` draws the grid (and fits labels into buttons); `js/speech.js` speaks (every sound in the app goes through it:
  word speed vs Sentence speed, and the message-bar playback with pauses).
- `js/critters.js` the critter's settings, checks and drawing; `js/modelprompt.js` shows the ring
  and critter in Model mode; `js/modelsettings.js` is the Model prompt part of the adult menu.
- `js/profiles.js` + `js/db.js` learner profiles and photos (IndexedDB);
  `js/storage.js` small settings (current learner, passcode) in localStorage.
- `js/setup.js` the quick setup card (first open and Add learner); `js/lock.js` passcode keypad (and the 10-minute unlock); `js/adult.js` adult menu; `js/editor.js` button editor;
  `js/hold.js` press-and-hold; `js/ui.js` small dialog helpers;
  `js/events.js` in-memory tap list for a later phase.
- `images/critters/` the five drawn animals and three accessories (original drawings; the body
  color is the CSS variable `--body`, so recoloring needs no extra files).
- `images/symbols/` the built-in symbols (one SVG per word); `data/image-sources.json`
  says where each came from. `js/backup.js` saves and restores backups.
- `data/word-forms.json` the word families for the Forms button; `data/dolch.json` the 318 Dolch words
  (only read by `tools/dolch-coverage.py`, which writes `docs/dolch-coverage.md`).
- `tools/make-credits.py` rewrites CREDITS.md from image-sources.json;
  `tools/update-cache-list.py` rewrites the file list in sw.js (run both after changing
  pictures or files, then bump `CACHE_VERSION`).
- `sw.js` caches the app for offline use. Change `CACHE_VERSION` whenever files change.
- `js/channel.js` says whether this copy is stable or beta (version label and storage prefix); `beta/` is the generated test copy (see How updates are tested).
